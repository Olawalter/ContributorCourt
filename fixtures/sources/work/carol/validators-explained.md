# Validators explained

Author wallet: 0xcc7edd6b8bcb5febcc62cf47b4923a8267831e56
Published: 2026-09-12

A leader validator proposes a result and the other validators repeat the​ work and vote on it under the equivalence rule.​ A majority accepts the result, and an appeal during the appeal window sends it to more validators.

Every validator​ has stake at risk, so each has a reason to check the leader's work rather than copy its vote.
